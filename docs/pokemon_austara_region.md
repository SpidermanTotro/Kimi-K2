# Pokémon Opal and Pokémon Jasper

## Welcome to the Austara Region

Embark on an epic adventure through the Austara Region in Pokémon Opal and Pokémon Jasper. Inspired by the diverse landscapes and unique wildlife of Australia, these new titles introduce Trainers to an expansive region filled with never-before-seen Pokémon and exciting challenges.

## Explore the Austara Region

The Austara Region features diverse environments ranging from sun-scorched deserts to lush rainforests and snow-capped mountains. Trainers will journey through:

### Canberra
The modern capital city where your adventure begins. Meet Professor Acacia and receive your first partner Pokémon at the region's premier research facility.

### The Great Coral Maze
An intricate network of coral reefs teeming with Water-type Pokémon. Explore underwater grottos and discover hidden treasures in this vibrant aquatic environment.

### Uluru's Heart
A sacred monolith in the desert's center, steeped in ancient mystery. This spiritual landmark holds the key to the region's legendary Pokémon.

### The Blue Mountains
Navigate treacherous mountain paths and deep valleys shrouded in a unique blue haze. Home to powerful Rock and Flying-type Pokémon.

### The Never-Never
A vast outback wilderness that tests even the most experienced Trainers. Hardy Ground and Fire-type Pokémon thrive in this harsh environment.

### The Daintree Jungle
An ancient rainforest with a dense canopy blocking sunlight. Bug and Grass-type Pokémon inhabit every level of this mysterious ecosystem.

### Melburn Artway
A vibrant city known for street art and cultural festivals. The premier destination for Pokémon Contests and fashion.

### The Snowy Slopes
A chilly alpine region where Ice-type Pokémon can be found. Challenge other Trainers in the snow or enjoy winter sports.

## Choose Your First Partner Pokémon

Begin your journey with one of three newly discovered Pokémon:

**Type:** Grass

## Discover New Pokémon

The Austara Region is home to many newly discovered Pokémon species:

**Wombatton**  
Type: Ground  
A sturdy Pokémon known for digging intricate burrow systems. Evolves into Womberon (Ground/Steel), which features a reinforced, metallic hide.

**Kookaburra**  
Type: Normal/Flying  
Its cheerful cry sounds like laughter. Evolves into Kookalaugh, which can mimic various sounds to confuse opponents.

**Dingoast**  
Type: Ground/Dark  
A cunning Pokémon that roams the outback in packs, using coordinated tactics to outmaneuver opponents.

**Echnida**  
Type: Electric/Ground  
Generates static electricity from its sharp quills. Can discharge powerful electric attacks when threatened.

**Tassie-Devil**  
Type: Dark  
Despite its small size, this fierce Pokémon possesses the strongest bite force of any small Pokémon. Evolves into Tazmaniac (Dark/Fighting).

**Thylavision**  
Type: Ghost/Normal  
A spectral Pokémon resembling the extinct Tasmanian Tiger. Rarely seen, its appearance is considered a mysterious omen.

**Corroborock**  
Type: Rock/Psychic  
Resembles ancient rock formations adorned with Aboriginal-style art. Said to hold the memories of the land itself.

## Legendary Pokémon

### Rainbow Serpent (Dragon/Fairy)

### Yowie-Gloom (Dark/Ground)

### Bunyip (Water/Ghost)

A mysterious Pokémon that guards the sacred waterholes of Austara. Legends say it protects these waters from those who would desecrate them.

## The Story

Your journey takes you to the heart of a conflict between Team Grasp and the Dreamkeepers.

**Team Grasp** is an organization that seeks to exploit the Austara Region's natural resources for profit. Led by the ruthless CEO Warren, they use advanced technology to capture rare Pokémon and mine sacred sites, threatening the region's delicate balance.

**The Dreamkeepers**, led by the wise elder Aunty Joy and her grandson Maliyan, work to preserve the ancient culture and environment of Austara. As you progress through your journey, you'll need to decide where you stand in this conflict and ultimately protect the region from those who would harm it.

## Gym Leaders and the Pokémon League

Challenge eight Gym Leaders across the Austara Region:

1. **Leif** (Cairns City) - Bug-type specialist
2. **Bronte** (Broken Hill) - Rock-type specialist  
3. **Kai** (Gold Coast) - Water-type specialist
4. **Shadow** (Tasmania Island) - Dark-type specialist
5. **Aurora** (The Snowy Slopes) - Ice-type specialist
6. **Ember** (Red Centre) - Fire-type specialist
7. **Skylar** (Blue Mountains) - Flying-type specialist
8. **Canberra** (Melburn Artway) - Fairy-type specialist

After collecting all eight Gym Badges, challenge the Elite Four and the Champion to become the Austara Region Champion.

## Experience Australian Culture

Pokémon Opal and Pokémon Jasper feature authentic Australian cultural elements woven throughout the adventure. Characters use distinctive Australian expressions and slang, while the themes of mateship (friendship) and giving everyone a fair go are central to the story. The soundtrack incorporates traditional instruments like the didgeridoo alongside modern compositions, creating a unique musical identity for the Austara Region.

## New Features

**Outback Survival**  
Navigate harsh desert environments where water supplies and weather conditions affect your journey. Choose your Pokémon wisely to overcome these challenges.

**Coral Diving**  
Explore vibrant underwater areas in the Great Coral Maze. Discover hidden grottos and encounter rare Water-type Pokémon in the depths.

**Pokémon Contests**  
Showcase your Pokémon's talents in Melburn Artway's Contest Hall. Compete in various categories and earn exclusive ribbons.

## Version Differences

**Pokémon Opal** and **Pokémon Jasper** each feature exclusive Pokémon and areas. Trade with friends to complete your Pokédex and experience everything the Austara Region has to offer.
